### Work items (one PR each; open an issue by REST first with the title given)

1. **"Perf budget as a gate"** (`perf/budget-gate`). `scripts/e2e-perf.mjs` gains
   `--budget` (fail when a moment exceeds its budget), a **longest main-thread block** column
   (Long Tasks via `PerformanceObserver`, plus the click handler's own time), a `report.json`,
   and runs in `qa.yml` after the playthroughs. Starting budgets are **measured on `main` the
   day the work starts** (the QA log's table predates #162 and #205; at #205 a week was about
   1.1 s and Skip 3.2 s under ×4) and set a little above those numbers, so the gate is green on
   day one; N8's suggested budgets (title 3 s, grounds 8 s, hand-off 5 s, a week 150 ms, bundle
   600 KB gzipped) are the floor each later item tightens toward. **Assumed (needs Leon):** the budgets; a
   failing budget fails the nightly job, not CI on PRs.
2. **"The multi-week advance yields between weeks"** (`perf/advance-yields`). `advanceUntil`
   becomes async: each week's tick stays one synchronous `advanceWeek()` (no hook can observe a
   half-run week), but the loop awaits an animation frame between weeks, so the page paints and
   takes taps. The HUD shows "Week 12 of 27…" on the Advance button, disables the other
   controls, and offers **Stop** (stops after the current week with the same toast as a rule
   stop). The stop rules (`advanceRules.ts`) and autosave are unchanged. Its only callers are
   the HUD through `main.ts` and `tests/core/advance.test.ts`; the soak and the playthrough
   reach it through `advanceWeek` and the HUD, so they need no change beyond awaiting it.
   Tests: fake timers show one frame per week and Stop between weeks; every
   `advanceRules` test unchanged; e2e: "To finals" shows the line, the page answers a tap
   mid-run. **Assumed:** one frame per week (not a fixed delay); Stop finishes the week in hand.
3. **"The fast-forwarded match plays in the Worker"** (`perf/match-worker`). Generalise #212's
   Worker into `matchWorker`: a job is `{ home, away, opts }`, the answer a `MatchResult`;
   `runMatch` is the only import. In a fast-forwarded week matchday holds the week
   (`ctx.hold()` now allowed under fast-forward inside the async advance), posts the player's
   match, and applies the result on `resumeWeek()`; the rival round (#212) shares the same
   Worker and queue. Node, tests and the soak have no Worker and play in place, as #212 does.
   Tests: the Worker's `MatchResult` equals in-place `runMatch` byte for byte on 20 seeds (the
   same check #212 made); the soak's results are unchanged; `e2e:perf`'s fast-forwarded week
   main-thread time drops under 300 ms. **Assumed:** one Worker for both; a failed Worker falls
   back in place with a console warning, never a different result.
4. **"Skip without a freeze"** (`perf/skip-worker`). Skip no longer runs the remaining ticks on
   the main thread. The match is deterministic from `{ seed, sheets, calls[], weather }`
   (coach-calls §2), so Skip posts that record to the Worker, which plays to full time and
   returns the `MatchResult`; the screen shows a short "Skipping…" beat, then the final
   whistle, score and the usual full-time flow. The resume record (step 2 of coach calls) is
   updated the same way. Tests: skipping at any tick gives the same `MatchResult` as playing
   on (golden, 20 seeds × 3 skip points); the playthrough's Skip step passes; `e2e:perf`'s Skip
   row under 300 ms of block. **Assumed:** re-simulate from the record rather than transfer the
   live state; no pop-ups during a skip (as today).
5. **"The hand-off paints ahead"** (`perf/handoff`, closes #154). The stadium, crowd and
   atmosphere layers are painted once per session and kept (as the `atm-*` textures already
   are); the two kit atlases for the next fixture (the opponent is known from the fixtures list)
   are painted during the week in idle time after `week:advanced` and cached by kit key, so
   `MatchScene.create()` finds them ready; if not (first match, a kit change), a one-frame
   "Kick-off…" beat lets the tap return before painting. Tests: the atlas cache keyed by kit
   design (a kit change repaints), the scene finds textures present, `e2e:perf` hand-off under
   1 s. **Assumed:** cache in memory only (textures are cheap to repaint on reload).
6. **"Boot paints what the first frame needs"** (`perf/boot-lazy`, QA finding 6; N8 called it
   a design change, so **Assumed (needs Leon)**). `BootScene` paints the tiles, the manager,
   decor and the facilities actually placed at their levels, then starts Grounds; the rest of
   the facility art (every `defId` at three levels) is painted on demand when first placed,
   upgraded or previewed in the Build menu, or in idle slices after the first frame. Tests: a
   facility texture requested before it is painted is painted then (no missing-texture
   frame); the playthrough's build, upgrade and kit steps pass; `e2e:perf` Start → grounds
   under 3 s.
7. **Status file and docs** ride with each PR: ARCHITECTURE §6's cost paragraph and BAK-207
   §3.5 updated; the before/after table in `docs/status/2026-10-perf.md`.
