### #206 [P1] iPhone Safari: tutorial blown up, Skip / Next off screen

- PR #207, merged after the checker approved. The checker's should-fixes went in
  before the merge: e2e:webkit fails fast on a taken port, cleans up on Ctrl-C, and
  checks the anchored step boosted. Still to do: Leon's check on a real iPhone.
- Cause, checked against the issue's numbers:
  - `index.html` had no `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, so iOS Safari's landscape
    boost (up to 956/440 = 2.17× on a 16 Pro Max) was free to inflate the DOM text.
  - The bubble had no height cap or internal scroll, and `body` is
    `overflow: hidden`, so nothing could bring the buttons back.
  - A text boost alone can't put the buttons *below* the screen, because the
    bubble is docked at the bottom and grows upward. The issue's numbers point
    to more than that:
    - The box is ~757 CSS px wide, which is 2.17 × (320 + padding and border)
      ≈ 770. The whole box was scaled, not just the text.
    - The 320px measure (not the 240px short-screen rule) means the page was laid
      out more than 500px tall.

    That matches Safari keeping a portrait-width layout zoomed 2.17× after the
    phone turns sideways.
- Fix:
  - `text-size-adjust: 100%` on `html`.
  - `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. iOS still allows pinch-zoom.
  - The tutorial bubble is capped to the viewport (`dvh`, with a `vh`
    fallback), its copy scrolls inside it, and Skip / Next never shrink.
  - The Guide scrolls only its page; tabs and foot (Replay, Close) stay in view.
    On short screens the tabs are one swipeable row and the foot one line.
  - Dialogs cap at `85dvh`.
- Test: `npm run e2e:webkit` (`scripts/e2e-webkit.mjs`) runs Playwright WebKit as
  an iPhone (touch, DPR 3, iOS UA) at 956×330 and 844×390. It checks
  text-size-adjust, then the bubble and the Guide, plain and with every font
  boosted 2.17×: inside the viewport, Skip / Next / Replay / Close on screen and
  hit by a tap, the copy scrolling. It fails 10 checks on main and passes with
  the fix. It runs in CI as a new `webkit` job. Unit tests pin the CSS
  (`tests/ui/fitViewport.test.ts`).
- Not verifiable here: WebKit on Linux has no text autosizer and no rotation
  zoom, so the real iPhone needs a check.
- Assumed (needs Leon):
  - `maximum-scale=1`: it stops pinch-zoom on Android Chrome, but iOS ignores
    it for pinch.
  - The CI `webkit` job downloads Playwright's WebKit on each PR (about a minute).
    The runner has none, and this is the only job that fetches a browser.
  - In this container, WebKit went into the scratchpad and its system
    libraries were added with apt; `/opt/pw-browsers` was not touched.
