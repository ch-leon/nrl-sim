## N11.2 Change the plan at the breaks

- `LiveSim.setTactics(side, tactics)`: a change of plan is an input at a tick, like a coach
  call (`plans: { tick, side, tactics }[]` beside `calls`), taken only at a break (half-time,
  or from a score until the kick-off; refused in open play and at a goal-line drop-out) and in
  force from the kick-off, with a commentary line. `runMatch`, `LivePlayback` and the live match
  record carry the list, so a resumed match and a replay play the same match. No change is
  exactly today's match (tests on `runMatch` and the whole watched match).
- On screen: a **half-time card** (the score, each side's legs, the calls paid off, the two
  rows of presets, Continue) holds the match; at ×4 and above it goes on after 8 s unless
  touched. A **Plan** chip beside the call chips opens the same rows from a score until the
  kick-off. Screens at both sizes: `docs/screenshots/n11-2/`.
- Harness: a scenario's `plans` list (`at`: `half-time`, `try`, `conceded`).
- Measured what each preset does over whole matches (tuning reference §27). Both ways round
  over 160 matches: **Compressed wins about 61%** against Slide, **All-out about 37%** and
  **Expansive about 35%** against Balanced. Now that the player picks, that is a dominant pick
  and two traps; raised as **#241**, not tuned here (the plan tables are not N11's).
- **Assumed (needs Leon):** half-time holds the clock for Continue (8 s auto-continue at ×4
