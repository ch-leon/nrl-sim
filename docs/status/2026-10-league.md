- **The start is the problem.** An idle manager wins 18–19 of 24 in season 1 off a
  +13 start, and still makes the eight nine seasons in ten for three seasons. Only

- **Hard already bites an idle manager** (70% sacked by season 5) through the board's
  finals target, not through the match; the fight is over patience, as the plan wants,
  but only once the start advantage has worn off.

## #143 Rivals develop in-season and the league mean holds

  rivals' own RNG stream (`rivalDevelopSeed`: hashed from the week and `rngSeed`,

## #146 Stars aren't free: signing fees by rating, and one marketWage

- PR #147, merged. No save bump.
- `signingFee(state, agent)` in `freeagency.ts`: Academy prospects still cost
  `SIGNING_FEE_WEEKS` (4) × wage; everyone else pays a transfer-style fee built on
  `contracts.transferFee` (overall² × 10 × an age factor: ×1.3 at 22 and under, ×0.3 at
  34+), less a Scout Office discount of 10% a level (`SCOUT_FEE_DISCOUNT_PER_LEVEL`).
  An 80-overall in his prime is about $64k, a 65 about $42k, a 55 about $30k; four
  weeks' wages made an 85 a $5k buy.
- The Recruit card shows the fee and how it was worked out ("by rating and age",
  "…, Scout Office −10%", "4×wage, Academy prospect"); the Sign button already showed
  the amount.
- `marketWage` now has one copy in `data/player.ts` (`WAGE_PER_OVERALL` 15 + `WAGE_BASE`
  100); `contracts.ts` and `rivals.ts` re-export it, `morale.ts` imports it instead of
  mirroring the formula, and both generators use it.
- Rivals competing for the pool: measured before adding anything, over 20 idle
  seasons, rivals already sign **94%** of the 65+ free agents the player leaves before
  the next refresh, **48%** the very next week (the player gets first look at every
  fresh pool for one week, then a rival with cap room takes the star). So no new
  "snap up" mechanism was added; the existing weekly signing is it.
- Not changed, outside N5's file list: the "Unsettled at a rival" event card
  (`systems/events.ts`) still quotes four weeks' wages to poach a rival's star. It is
  rare (cooldown, needs the fee in hand) but it is the last cheap star; a one-line
  change to `signingFee` there would close it (Needs Leon / N8).
- Verified: tsc, vitest (fee by overall and age, juniors unchanged, the Scout Office
  discount, one marketWage), build, the full soak (no problems), Playwright at 844×390
  and 1280×800 (`docs/screenshots/146/`, Recruit with the new fee line).
