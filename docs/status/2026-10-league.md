What it says:

- **The start is the problem.** An idle manager wins 18–19 of 24 in season 1 off a
  +13 start, and still makes the eight nine seasons in ten for three seasons. Only
  ageing (no training built) and the rivals' rollover development close the gap, by
  about 3 points a season; by season 5 the idle club is a mid-table side and the
  board has sacked 30% of them.
- **Stars are free.** The managed club buys a 73-overall in season 1 and 83–87s from
  then on for $4–5k each (four weeks' wages), reaching a best nine of 89 and winning
  the premiership every season by season 5. Its fees total $14k a season.
- **The league mean drifts up**, 57 → 65 over five seasons, with `RIVAL_LEAGUE_MEAN`
  at 55: rollover development (young players +0–5 a year) and juniors anchored above
  the weak clubs lift every rival, and the 10% pull is not enough.
- **Hard already bites an idle manager** (70% sacked by season 5) through the board's
  finals target, not through the match; the fight is over patience, as the plan wants,
  but only once the start advantage has worn off.
