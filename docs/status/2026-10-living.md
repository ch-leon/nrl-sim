distinct attacks / distinct defences. A style more than 4 points from even at
a level rating, or more than 4 points from the strength-adjusted mean, is

## N14.3 The cascade and the contracts in debt (#282)

- PR #285, merged. **Save v18** (the board loan). Built on N12.3 (#277, v17) and
  N8's #278 (forced sales keep 13 fit).
- **(a) One forced sale a week.** Under the −$100,000 floor the club sells its highest-paid
  player who can go (#278's rule: a fit player only while more than 13 are fit), once a
  week. Before, it sold everyone down to the minimum in one week: a sale pays nothing, so
  BAK-188's "until money ≥ floor" was never what happened. The club now sinks in steps,
  and the multi-week advance stops each week with the name (N12.5's stop).
- **(b) The board loan.** A renewal the club can't afford goes on a loan from the board:
  - at contract end, the four weeks' wage; an early renewal, its fee;
  - it comes in on the board's line (`board loan`), so the books still sum;
  - it is repaid in full at the rollover, after the prize and before the board's verdict (so
    a club is not "solvent" on borrowed money, the checker's note), on the same
    line (`board loan repaid`);
  - at most `BOARD.LOAN_CAP` ($50,000) a season; past that, a contract ends as before;
  - the Contracts screen's Renew says "on a board loan", the toast names the loan, and the
    Finance P&L notes it under the board's row.
- **(c) The Academy call-up.** Before a round the club would forfeit (fewer than 13 fit),
  the Academy calls up juniors to make the side:
  - generated at the Academy's level (no Academy: the lowest band) and signed at the junior
    wage to the season's end, with no fee;
  - a toast names them;
  - the multi-week advance still stops the week before ("Only N fit (need 13) — the Academy
    will call up juniors unless you sign players"), so the manager can sign someone better;
  - a week with 13 fit draws no dice, so those weeks play as before.
- **Measured** (`npm run soak -- --difficulties easy,normal,hard --jobs 4`, 20 seeds × 5
