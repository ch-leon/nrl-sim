  atlas and the two player atlases. The same pixels as the scene painted before.
  `scenes/matchArtAhead.ts` looks up the player's next fixture after each week, and when a
  game starts or loads. The art worker (`src/art/match/artWorker.ts`) paints it and
  transfers the buffers back, then the cache uploads them one texture per idle slice.
  - One painting is kept, keyed by the field offset and both kits. A kit change or
    another opponent paints again, and frees the old one's textures.
  - Nothing is painted during a multi-week run or under a match (the art on screen is
    that fixture's, and Skip needs the cores). An upload that was waiting out a match
    waits 1.5 s more, while the club screens start.
- **The ground once.** The ground is about 90% of the background's cost (350–400 ms of
  about 430 in Node) and the same for every fixture. `stadium.ts` keeps it, and each
  background starts from a copy: a test checks the same pixels. The art worker paints it
  while the title screen is up (`warmMatchArt()`, one additive line in `main.ts`).
- **The scene.** `MatchScene.create()` takes the painting and only places it. The effects,
  atmosphere and playback start on the next frame, so no frame does it all.
  - If not everything is up (the first match of a session, a kit just changed), a live
    match shows "Kick-off…" while the worker finishes. The rest then goes up one texture
    a frame.
  - The lab paints in place, as before.
- **512 px chunks.** A ×4 phone in software GL spends about 0.3 s uploading a megapixel,
  so a 1024² chunk was a 300 ms frame. A 512² chunk is about 80 ms.
- **Checker (a separate reviewer subagent): approved.** Taken up from its notes: a
  scene shut down during "Kick-off…" now frees the textures already uploaded for it
  (it adopts them when it takes the painting), and a test puts the chunks back
  together and compares every byte with the layer.
- **Fixed on the way:** the kick-off path's first check asked `isActive()` inside
  `create()`, where the scene is still CREATING. A match whose art was partly uploaded
  then never built (the probe hung at a 4 s tap). Shutdown bumps a token, and that is the
  guard now.

  Budgets: `handoffMs` 1 s, `handoffBlockMs` 300 ms.
- **Not met: a first match with the art not painted yet, under 1 s.** Reached 1.56 s.
  - The blocker is the worker's paint time. On a ×4 phone the kit-dependent art (stands,
    hoardings, crowd, two player atlases, on the kept ground) is about 1.2 s of worker
    time; the art lands about 1.25 s after a 1 s tap, then about 0.3 s of uploads.
  - Painting it on the main thread instead would freeze for about 0.65 s.
  - Under 1 s would need either a cheaper stadium (an art change, which the plan rules
