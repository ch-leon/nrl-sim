## Phone performance budget

`npm run e2e:perf` (`scripts/e2e-perf.mjs`, PERFPR): Chromium 141 headless at 844×390 with
CDP CPU throttling ×4 as the stand-in for a mid-range phone, software GL. Click handlers are
timed inside the page (`performance.now()` around `el.click()`), so a synchronous handler such
as the week tick is measured on its own; the async moments are click-to-condition. `main` at
ef4c501, classic engine (the live-match setting off), a new game.

| Moment (CPU ×4, 844×390) | Time |
|---|---|
| Page load → title screen | 2.1 s |
| Start → grounds (every grounds texture painted, HUD up) | 6.1 s |
| Advance Week on a match week → match scene | 3.8 s, of which 3.1 s is the click itself (the match textures painted inside it, #154) |
| Skip → full time | 0.7 s |
| Back to Club → grounds | 1.3 s |
| A week of a ×4 advance (fast-forward, classic engine) | 76 ms (6 weeks in 458 ms) |
| To finals / to season end, per call | 36–383 ms; the rollover about 90 ms |
| Grounds frame rate | 8 fps under software GL (JS 5% of the frame; a real phone's GPU does the rest) |
| Bundle | 1883 KB raw, 485 KB gzipped: one 1875 KB chunk (Phaser and the game), two tiny PWA scripts |

Reading: the club loop is cheap (a week under 100 ms, the rollover under 100 ms); the two
expensive moments are texture painting at boot (finding 6) and at every match hand-off
(finding 5, #154). The script measures and prints; it does not fail on a budget. Unthrottled
desktop Chromium is roughly 4× faster on every row.
