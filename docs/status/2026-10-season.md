## #234 Debt stops the advance once (item 5)

- PR #239, merged. No `GameState` change, no save bump, no balance change; nothing in
  `src/match/**`, `matchday.ts` or the QA files. QA finding 10: in debt, every multi-week
  advance stopped after one week on the economy's weekly `bankruptcy:warning`, so a club in
  the red could not fast-forward (N8's playthrough budgeted one call a week, #215).
- **The rule** (`core/advanceRules.ts`, pure): `WeekFacts` loses `bankruptcyWarned` and gains
  `moneyBefore`, `moneyAfter` and `forcedSales`; `debtStop`, read first by `postWeekStop`,
  stops on a forced sale this week ("Forced sale: J. Smith sold to cover debts."; several:
  three names "and n more"), on the balance first passing the fire-sale floor ("Balance below
  the fire-sale floor (-$100,000)."), and on the crossing into the red (≥ $0 before the week,
  < $0 after: "Club has gone into debt."). One stop a week, the most pressing first; a club
  that stays in the red without crossing anything runs on to its target.
- **The facts** (`core/advance.ts`): the money from the before/after snapshots the loop
  already takes; the forced sales from `bankruptcy:warning`, which gains one optional field
  `forcedSales?: string[]` and is emitted after the sale loop so it can name the players
  (before/after state cannot tell a sale apart: no fee moves the balance, expiring contracts
  leave in the same tick, rivals or a re-roll can take the player from the pool the same
  week). The warning still fires once a week in the red with the same toasts in the same
  order; #225's yielding loop, progress and Stop are untouched.
- **HUD:** below $0 the money tile keeps its red class and gains a white-on-red "in debt" tag
  from 760 px wide; narrower (the 667×375 phone) the tag is hidden from sight but still read
  by screen readers and the tile wears a red border instead, so the HUD stays one row (60 px;
  the first cut wrapped it to 93 px, N8's must-fix). The debt line is read from
  `ECON.BANKRUPTCY_WARN` in the rule, the HUD and Finance alike.
  **Finance:** one field, `weeksToFloor` (the first whole week that leaves the balance below
  the floor at the current burn), one row "Fire sale: in about N weeks at this burn" ("below
  the floor now"), only when in debt and burning.
- **Docs:** the Guide (Advancing further, Finance), ARCHITECTURE §5 and the events listing,
  BAK-188's bankruptcy cascade; a note for N8 in `docs/status/2026-10-qa.md` ("Changes to the
