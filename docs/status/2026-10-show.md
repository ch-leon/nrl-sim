- **The zone's bounds come from the rule file.** `matchField.bonusZone(dir)` is the in-goal
  between the uprights at the end a side attacking `dir` scores at, in sim metres: try line
  to dead-ball line (`IN_GOAL`, 10 m), `MID_Y ± GOALS.halfPosts` (5.5 m wide); a test sweeps
  the in-goal on a 0.25 m grid and checks that inside the rectangle is exactly where

## #293 Commentary for the live sim (item 2)

- PR #294, merged (8601af8). No `GameState` change, no save bump, no match-sim change: the commentary
  reads the events `live.ts` emits and picks its lines by a hash, never a dice roll.
- **The lines.** A new `src/match/view/commentary.ts` keyed by the kinds the live sim emits
  (51: the 50 `this.emit` kinds and the pushed `call-paid`), sharpened by their detail
  where the line changes with it: each kick type (long, bomb, grubber, chip, cross-field)
  and how it comes down (taken, scooped up, regathered, spilled, into touch, dead, on the
  full, the 40/20, charged down), the penalty calls (offside, high; the shot, the tap, the
  kick for touch), six again, the bonus try and every kick at goal good or missed, fends,
  steps, busts, the wrestle won (`break-tackle:wrestle`, the quick play-the-ball) and lost
  (the tackle, the slow one), offloads, the four sin-bin reasons and the return,
  interchanges, the coach's calls and plan changes, the siren, half-time (who leads, or
  level), golden point and the final whistle (a win, a draw, golden point): 82 moments,
  three or four lines each, 250 lines (and four story moments for the report). The words
  are the pop-ups' and the Guide's (six again, tap and go, the 40/20, the sin bin, the
  drop-out, dummy-half, play-the-ball).
- **Seeded, not rolled.** A moment's line is a hash of the match seed, the tick, the
  moment and its place in the tick; a `Commentator` follows the match event by event and
  the ball tick by tick, so it knows a kick is in the air and never says the same line
  twice running. A kick caught reads as a catch; a kick that came to ground (flat on it two
  ticks running: the end of its flight, then a bounce or a ball lying loose) and is picked
  up reads as a scoop, as the sim words it (#294's checker: 10% of scoops had read as
  catches; over 16 matches after the fix, 345 kicks taken and 253 balls scooped, none
  misread). It hears every event, said or not: a resumed match's silent stretch and a Skip
  played here go through it unseen, so the match reads the same however it is watched.
  The whistles hash the seed alone, so a match skipped in the Worker ends on the line it
  would have ended on (`fullTime`, from the result's score).
- **The ticker reads it.** `LivePlayback` says each event's line through the overlay's
  `comment()`; the overlay keeps the score from the events and says nothing of its own
  (its seed and rng went with the old templates; `MatchScene` stops passing a seed). The
  coach's calls and plan changes, which the ticker used to read as the sim's raw text,
  have lines of their own. Skip shows "Skipping to full time…" and then the final whistle.
- **The report's summary.** `buildMatchReport` gains `summary`: the full-time line and the
  story of the tries (a man with two or more, tries to tries, apiece, or none), in the same

- **Width.** The ticker at 844×390 (and at 1280×800, the same 34rem strip) is 440 px of

## #297 Kicks go wide: the camera and the trail (item 3)

- PR #301, open. No `GameState` change, no save bump, no match-sim change: the view reads
  the ball in each snapshot and the events `live.ts` emits; nothing in `src/match/league/**`

- **Where it lands, read off the ball.** The snapshot carries the ball, not its flight, but
  the sim flies a kick at a steady pace over the ground on a parabola in time
  (`core/ball.ts`), so a kick's first three ticks in the air fix where it comes down and how
  high it goes (`predictFlight`). Over two matches every non-grubber kick (55 of 55) was read
  by its third tick, and every reading equals the spot the sim aimed it at to a millionth of
  a metre (a test reads the sim's own flight to check). A grubber's hops are not a parabola:
  its framing holds the kicker and the ball as it goes.
- **The framing.** From that third tick the camera holds where it comes down, the kicker
  where he struck it, and the top of its flight, at the widest whole-number zoom from the
  beat's down to 1 that holds them inside the band the overlay leaves clear (76 px under the
  scoreboard, 120 over the ticker and speed buttons, 40 on the left, 160 on the right for
  the Stats panel). When they cannot all fit even at 1, the landing stays in the band (40 px
  in from its edges, room for the men under it and their rings), then the kicker, then the
  top as far as it goes (a bomb's ball climbs out under the scoreboard on a phone). Over two
  matches (55 kicks read): at 844×390 every kick but a chip goes from zoom 2 to 1 (17
  kick-offs, 20 long kicks, 9 bombs, 5 cross-field kicks; 4 chips stay at 2); at 1280×800 the
  cross-field kicks go to 1 (4 of 5) and one long kick of 20, and the rest stay at 2 and only
  the centre moves. The landing was in the band in every framing (7,006, a tick each at both
  sizes), and the kicker with it in 6,422 of them, 91.7%: every one at 1280×800, and 2,919 of
  3,503 at 844×390 (83%), where a long kick keeps him in 648 of 1,170 framings (55%) and a
  cross-field kick in 248 of 310 (80%), every other kind always. (#301's checker: the PR had
