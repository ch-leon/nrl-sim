## #281 The bonus zone on the pitch and the Nines notes (item 1)

- PR #289, merged (5f39031). No `GameState` change, no save bump, no match-sim change (no new
  dice; the sim's events are read as they are).
- **The zone's bounds come from the rule file.** `matchField.bonusZone(dir)` is the in-goal
  between the uprights at the end a side attacking `dir` scores at, in sim metres: try line
  to dead-ball line (`IN_GOAL`, 10 m), `MID_Y ± GOALS.halfPosts` (5.5 m wide); a test sweeps
  the in-goal on a 0.25 m grid and checks that inside the rectangle is exactly where
  `inBonusZone` makes a try worth five. `bonusZoneGrid` maps it onto the iso grid for the
  art, `bonusZoneCorners` onto the screen for a layer.
- **The pitch art.** `stadium.ts` paints each zone as a lighter band over the in-goal grass
  (24% toward a pale lime, the stripes kept) with a thin dashed edge on its three open
  sides, a blend of the line colour that is never the try line's pure white and half its
  thickness (a test: no pure-white pixel inside the zone; the band is lighter than the
  in-goal beside it). The ground is painted once per session (N10.5), so it costs nothing
  per match.
- **The uprights moved onto the zone's sides.** The art's posts stood 1.8 tiles apart
  (about 6.8 m) against the rule's 5.5 m (`GOALS.halfPosts`), so the zone painted between
  them would have been narrower than the posts. `POST_GY` now comes from the zone, the
  crossbar keeps a clean 2:1 step (`CROSSBAR_DX = −2·CROSSBAR_DY`), and the post shadows
  follow. A kick the sim calls wide now passes outside the drawn posts too.
- **The BONUS TRY! pop-up points at it.** Its second line reads FROM THE ZONE (the ticker
  names the scorer), and a new view layer, `zoneLayer.ts`, lights the zone the try was
  grounded in: the end is read off where the scorer stands on the try's tick, the glow is a
  pale-gold polygon on the ground (depth −85,000: over the field, under the shadows and
  every sprite), lit at once, pulsing gently for 4.5 s real and fading over the last 0.9 s.
  It lasts past the overlay's try banner (3.2 s) on purpose: the banner sits over the
  scorer at both sizes, so the pop-up under it is never seen (screenshot
  `844x390-bonus-try.png`), and the glow is what is left pointing at the zone when it goes.
  Pure over the event and the snapshot: no dice, nothing the sim reads. Skipped matches
  light nothing.
- **A bonus try's conversion folds into its banner** (`matchOverlay.ts`, one line): the

## #293 Commentary for the live sim (item 2)

- PR #294, open. No `GameState` change, no save bump, no match-sim change: the commentary
  reads the events `live.ts` emits and picks its lines by a hash, never a dice roll.
- **The lines.** A new `src/match/view/commentary.ts` keyed by the kinds the live sim emits
  (55: the 54 `this.emit` kinds and the pushed `call-paid`), sharpened by their detail
  where the line changes with it: each kick type (long, bomb, grubber, chip, cross-field)
  and how it comes down (taken, scooped up, regathered, spilled, into touch, dead, on the
  full, the 40/20, charged down), the penalty calls (offside, high; the shot, the tap, the
  kick for touch), six again, the bonus try and every kick at goal good or missed, fends,
  steps, busts, the wrestle won (`break-tackle:wrestle`, the quick play-the-ball) and lost
  (the tackle, the slow one), offloads, the four sin-bin reasons and the return,
  interchanges, the coach's calls and plan changes, the siren, half-time (who leads, or
  level), golden point and the final whistle (a win, a draw, golden point): 82 moments,
  three or four lines each, 250 lines (and four story moments for the report). The words
  are the pop-ups' and the Guide's (six again, tap and go, the 40/20, the sin bin, the
  drop-out, dummy-half, play-the-ball).
- **Seeded, not rolled.** A moment's line is a hash of the match seed, the tick, the
  moment and its place in the tick; a `Commentator` follows the match event by event so
  it knows a kick is in the air (a catch reads as one) and never says the same line twice
  running. It hears every event, said or not: a resumed match's silent stretch and a Skip
  played here go through it unseen, so the match reads the same however it is watched.
  The whistles hash the seed alone, so a match skipped in the Worker ends on the line it
  would have ended on (`fullTime`, from the result's score).
- **The ticker reads it.** `LivePlayback` says each event's line through the overlay's
  `comment()`; the overlay keeps the score from the events and says nothing of its own
  (its seed and rng went with the old templates; `MatchScene` stops passing a seed). The
  coach's calls and plan changes, which the ticker used to read as the sim's raw text,
  have lines of their own. Skip shows "Skipping to full time…" and then the final whistle.
- **The report's summary.** `buildMatchReport` gains `summary`: the full-time line and the
  story of the tries (a man with two or more, tries to tries, apiece, or none), in the same
  voice, seeded by the result. The card shows it under the score and drops its last line
  while the card would scroll (at 844×390 both lines fit: no scroll in either shot).
- **The old templates are gone:** `src/sim/commentary.ts` and its test deleted; the sin-bin
  test speaks through the new module; ARCHITECTURE §2's tree lists `commentary.ts` under
  `match/view/`; BAK-206's polish line ("commentary lines for fend / break-tackle / kick
  types") is marked done.
- **Width.** The ticker at 844×390 (and at 1280×800, the same 34rem strip) is 440 px of
  0.8rem Courier New at 7.68 px a character: 57 characters, measured in Chromium.
  `TICKER_FIT_CHARS` is 56, and every line filled with the longest names the game makes (a
  20-character player, `Brayden Quartermaine`; a club of the ladder's 16 characters; the
  longest call and plan; two-digit scores) fits it (a test). Clubs are named by their last
  word ("Larrikins"; "Zone Test FC" whole when its last word is short), as the report's
  columns are; a club named longer than 16 characters with a short last word is cut by the
  ticker's ellipsis, as before.
- Tests (`tests/match/view/commentary.test.ts`, new): the kinds read off `live.ts` itself,
  each with lines (a kind N13 adds without lines fails it) and a sample of every kind and
  detail said in our words; three or more different lines a moment; the words of the
  pop-ups and the Guide; kicks by type, calls, the bonus try, good and missed; a caught kick
  against a loose ball; the whistles from the score; the same seed giving the same lines
