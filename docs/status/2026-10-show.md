## #281 The bonus zone on the pitch and the Nines notes (item 1)

- PR #PR, open. No `GameState` change, no save bump, no match-sim change (no new dice; the
  sim's events are read as they are).
- **The zone's bounds come from the rule file.** `matchField.bonusZone(dir)` is the in-goal
  between the uprights at the end a side attacking `dir` scores at, in sim metres: try line
  to dead-ball line (`IN_GOAL`, 10 m), `MID_Y ± GOALS.halfPosts` (5.5 m wide); a test sweeps
  the in-goal on a 0.25 m grid and checks that inside the rectangle is exactly where
  `inBonusZone` makes a try worth five. `bonusZoneGrid` maps it onto the iso grid for the
  art, `bonusZoneCorners` onto the screen for a layer.
- **The pitch art.** `stadium.ts` paints each zone as a lighter band over the in-goal grass
  (24% toward a pale lime, the stripes kept) with a thin dashed edge on its three open
  sides, a blend of the line colour that is never the try line's pure white and half its
  thickness (a test: no pure-white pixel inside the zone; the band is lighter than the
  in-goal beside it). The ground is painted once per session (N10.5), so it costs nothing
  per match.
- **The uprights moved onto the zone's sides.** The art's posts stood 1.8 tiles apart
  (about 6.8 m) against the rule's 5.5 m (`GOALS.halfPosts`), so the zone painted between
  them would have been narrower than the posts. `POST_GY` now comes from the zone, the
  crossbar keeps a clean 2:1 step (`CROSSBAR_DX = −2·CROSSBAR_DY`), and the post shadows
  follow. A kick the sim calls wide now passes outside the drawn posts too.
- **The BONUS TRY! pop-up points at it.** Its second line reads FROM THE ZONE (the ticker
  names the scorer), and a new view layer, `zoneLayer.ts`, lights the zone the try was
  grounded in: the end is read off where the scorer stands on the try's tick, the glow is a
  pale-gold polygon on the ground (depth −85,000: over the field, under the shadows and
  every sprite), lit at once, pulsing gently for 4.5 s real and fading over the last 0.9 s.
  It lasts past the overlay's try banner (3.2 s) on purpose: the banner sits over the
  scorer at both sizes, so the pop-up under it is never seen (screenshot
  `844x390-bonus-try.png`), and the glow is what is left pointing at the zone when it goes.
  Pure over the event and the snapshot: no dice, nothing the sim reads. Skipped matches
  light nothing.
- **A bonus try's conversion folds into its banner** (`matchOverlay.ts`, one line): the
  banner folded a conversion only into a banner headed TRY!, so after a BONUS TRY! the
  conversion replaced it.
- **History.** A season played before the Nines rules carries a note under its row:
  "Before Nines: 80-minute matches, no bonus tries" (`history.ts`, `playedBeforeNines` /
  `fmtRulesNote`). Derived, not stored: the switch-over (#162, 20 Oct 08:23) came after the
  season review (save v12, #193, 06:08 the same morning), and every season recorded since
  has a review, so a season without one was played before it. The corpus by season (a
  test): v9, v10 and v11's season 1 noted (the v9 playthrough's players made 2.3 tackles a
  game, the old engine's rate, against 9–12 on the live sim); v12 to v17's not (the v12
  playthrough had the live match setting on: 9.6 tackles a game).
- **The Guide.** How Nines works gains "The bonus zone": a paragraph (5.5 m wide, 10 m deep,
  a lighter band with a dashed edge, worth 5, lights up when one is scored, a carrier with
  nobody near him goes round for it) and a picture (`zonePicture.ts`): one end from above,
  the zone drawn the way the pitch paints it, 5 in it and 4 either side. Built from
  `NINES_FACTS`, whose two new numbers (`postGapM`, `inGoalM`) a test keeps equal to
  `GOALS.halfPosts` and `IN_GOAL`. `GuideSection` gains an optional `figure`.
