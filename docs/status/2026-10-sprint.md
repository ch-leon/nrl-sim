### #72: grubbers on the classic engine (#156)

## Sin bins and professional fouls (#71, #191)

A penalty can send the offender to the sin bin (`league/sinbin.ts`, tuning reference §22):
5% anywhere, 25% inside his own 20 (a professional foul) plus 20% for each earlier penalty
his side conceded there, 30% for a high shot, +5% a level of Hitman or Hothead; never two
off a side. He jogs to the touchline and stands there for **five minutes of match clock**;
his side plays eight (the line, markers, chasers, support and pass options all run one
short; the lab's label reads `8 v 9`), stand-ins take his duties (the five-eighth kicks,
the prop plays dummy-half, a winger drops back) and a defence a man short is a little
easier to bust and step. **SIN BIN!** over him with the reason in the commentary, **BACK ON**
when he returns; `sin-bin` / `sin-bin-return` on the contract, a `sinBins` tally in the
player stats, −2 for Player of the Match. The roll is made on every penalty whatever the
chance, so bins turned off play the same match until a bin would have happened, and with
