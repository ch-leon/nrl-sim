  most 2.5 s, and grounding it 2 m short of the dead-ball line. About one try in twenty
  is a bonus try; a 3 m threshold doubles them for strong sides (`goals.bonusClearM`).

  60/50/35/15% by zone, out on the full about one in ten; 40/20 attempted by 8% of kickers on
  the last inside their own 40 (+20% a level of Big Boot or Playmaker), about 0.1 a match;
  a 40/20 is a tap restart (Nines); touch restarts are a play-the-ball 10 m in (no scrums, as
