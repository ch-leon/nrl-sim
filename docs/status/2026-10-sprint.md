### 2c — conversions, field goals (#121) and bonus-zone tries

Conversions are Nines drop kicks in line with the try, the kicker stepping back to where
the posts look widest; field goals late with the scores close (charge-down 8%; a miss is a
20 m tap until 2d). Bonus zone: a try grounded between the posts is worth 5, and a carrier
over the line with nobody near goes round under the posts for it.
