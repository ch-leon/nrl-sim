## Assumed (needs Leon)

  most 2.5 s, and grounding it 2 m short of the dead-ball line. About one try in twenty
  is a bonus try; a 3 m threshold doubles them for strong sides (`goals.bonusClearM`).
- **2d, how often kicks go for touch, 40/20 attempts, set pieces:** touch-finders from deep
  60/50/35/15% by zone, out on the full about one in ten; 40/20 attempted by 8% of kickers on
  the last inside their own 40 (+20% a level of Big Boot or Playmaker), about 0.1 a match;
  a 40/20 is a tap restart (Nines); touch restarts are a play-the-ball 10 m in (no scrums, as
  agreed); a set piece stops the clock.
- **2e, penalties:** rates per §17 (about 1% of rucks for six again, 0.8–2% offside by

  rare); a penalty after the siren that isn't a shot ends the half; no sin bins yet.

  (rain errors land above the §7 band, about 21 a match; the band is for a dry day); wind
  +12% kick length ±15%, goal kicks 1.4× wider; the old engine's −20% breaks in rain is not
  carried over. Injuries: the old engine's roll scaled down so a season's injuries stay at
  about 0.3 a match (the live sim has 3½× the big hits). A hurt man always comes off at the
  next stoppage, even with the rotation off.
