## #181 Build label and a visible update

- PR #182, branch `ux/build-label-update`.
- **Build label:** `vite.config.ts` fills a `__NRL_BUILD__` define with the UTC build
  date and the short SHA (`git rev-parse --short HEAD`, else `GITHUB_SHA`, else `local`);
  `src/ui/buildInfo.ts` reads it (`dev` without the define). The title screen's last
  line reads `Build 2026-10-19 979cbbe` (`[data-nrl-build]`). The Guide shows it too once
  item 4 lands.
- **Update prompt:** the service worker moves from `autoUpdate` to `prompt`. A new
  build raises a bar above the nav (`.nrl-update-toast`: "New version ready",
  Reload, Later) through `src/ui/updatePrompt.ts`; `src/fx/pwa.ts` also asks the
  browser for a new worker every 15 minutes and whenever the app comes back to the
  foreground. The bar never shows during a match: the scheduler waits for
  `body.nrl-match-active` to clear (MutationObserver) and the stylesheet hides it if a
  match starts while it is up. Later removes it until the next launch.
- For N8's playthrough: `?e2e` exposes `window.__nrlUpdateReady()`, which raises the
  bar the way a deploy would; the bar is `.nrl-update-toast`, its buttons
  `.nrl-update-reload` and `.nrl-update-later`.
- No GameState change, so no save bump.
- `docs/QA-pwa.md`: a manual check for the label and the bar; the autoUpdate note
  replaced.
- Verified: tsc, vitest (scheduler, label format, update checks with fake timers),
  build, Playwright at 844×390 and 1280×800 (title with the label; the bar on the
  grounds; no bar during a live match, the bar after Back to Club).
- Assumed (needs Leon): a prompt rather than an auto-update; the 15-minute check
  interval plus a check on returning to the app; Later hides the bar until the next
  launch; the label is date + short SHA (no time), `local` for a build without git.

## #185 Player page

- PR #TBD, branch `ux/player-page`.
- **The page** (`src/ui/screens/player.ts`, a dialog over the screen it came from):
  portrait in the club's kit (the training top for a free agent), name and overall, the
  five stat bars, every trait with its name, level and tip, age, position and fitness,
  morale as a word with why it is down (paid under the market, a contract running down,
  injured), injury risk, the contract as paid (the difficulty's wage multiplier applied,
  as the Contracts screen shows it), tenure, this season's and the career tallies.
- **Actions** where they apply, through the existing system functions: a free agent's
  Sign (the Recruit screen's fee and basis; disabled with a reason when the squad is
  full or the money short); one of yours gets Renew (fee and the new weekly wage; off
  when the contract is already full length), Sell (+fee) and Release (the settlement),
  the last two guarded by a second tap ("Really sell?") and by the squad minimum. The
  page follows the player through state changes: a renewal updates the deal line, a
  sale, release or signing closes it. A rival's player is read-only under the rival's name.
- **Tap targets:** the top row of every `PlayerCard` (portrait, name, badges; 48px tall,
  a › at the right, keyboard-reachable) opens the page on Squad and Recruit; the rows of
  a rival squad (now 44px tall) open it, and Close brings the squad back. The Squad
  screen's structure is unchanged beyond that one option on the card.
- For N8: `[data-player-open=<id>]` on a card's top row and on rival rows,
  `[data-player-page=<id>]` for the dialog, `[data-player-action=sign|renew|sell|release]`,
  `.nrl-player-close`.
- No GameState change, so no save bump.
- Verified: tsc, vitest (the view-model: words, tallies, each context's actions and their
  guards, morale reasons), build, Playwright at 844×390 and 1280×800
  (`docs/screenshots/185/`): a squad card and its page, a renewal, the armed Sell, the
  sale closing the page, a free agent's page, a rival squad's rows and a rival's page.
- Assumed (needs Leon): a dialog over the list rather than a full screen; the two-tap
  guard instead of a confirm dialog (a dialog would replace the page); morale words at
  70/50/30; the Contracts screen stays as it is (the page is the second way to the same
  actions).
