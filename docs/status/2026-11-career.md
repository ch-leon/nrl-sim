## #569 — execution contract and prototype calibration

- Audited current main at `9985b290181bbff2080305638963a3b1dfda72a3`, rather than
  the original concept's v23 baseline. Player schema/ceilings/XP/academy have
  merged; finance remains undelivered and its numerical decisions remain open.
- Published [the versioned contract](../specs/career-competitions.md), with source
  impact map, state/save ownership, finite decider, interval/finalisation rules,
  integrated rollover and an issue/dependency map.
- Structural prototype: 25 intervals, at most 23 player matches; 168 league + 9
  league-final + 23 cup + 24 trial = 224 world fixtures for full participation.
  This arithmetic does not establish actual phone duration or economic balance.
- Proposed data/counts are explicit. Annual cash/cap and non-league XP values
  remain inputs owned by their existing finance/progression issues; no fake
  calibration or approval is recorded.
- Validation: documentation links, slot coverage/uniqueness, 14-round pairing
  arithmetic, eight byes and the 224-fixture world total checked successfully.
  Source review clarified separate idempotent reward settlement, an exact tied
  cohort for head-to-head points, and both legacy week and fixture-round bounds.
- Current work is an isolated PR branch. Runtime calendar/tier/reward activation,
  source validation changes and live playtesting are subsequent deliverables.
