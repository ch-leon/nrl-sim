## #569 — execution contract and prototype calibration

- Audited current main at `9985b290181bbff2080305638963a3b1dfda72a3`, rather than
  the original concept's v23 baseline. Player schema/ceilings/XP/academy have
  merged; finance remains undelivered and its numerical decisions remain open.
- Published [the versioned contract](../specs/career-competitions.md), with source
  impact map, state/save ownership, finite decider, interval/finalisation rules,
  integrated rollover and an issue/dependency map.
- Structural prototype: 25 intervals, at most 23 player matches; 168 league + 9
  league-final + 23 cup + 24 trial = 224 world fixtures for full participation.
  This arithmetic does not establish actual phone duration or economic balance.
- Proposed data/counts are explicit. Annual cash/cap and non-league XP values
  remain inputs owned by their existing finance/progression issues; no fake
  calibration or approval is recorded.
- Validation: documentation links, slot coverage/uniqueness, 14-round pairing
  arithmetic, eight byes and the 224-fixture world total checked successfully.
  Source review clarified separate idempotent reward settlement, an exact tied
  cohort for head-to-head points, and both legacy week and fixture-round bounds.
- Current work is an isolated PR branch. Runtime calendar/tier/reward activation,
  source validation changes and live playtesting are subsequent deliverables.

## #571 — structural calendar and guarded results

- [PR #587](https://github.com/ch-leon/nrl-sim/pull/587), stacked on the state
  boundary, adds format v26 and explicit actual-DEV activation, independent slots,
  monotonic elapsed intervals and canonical player/rival fixture completion.
- Results own one-time stats, XP and Player-of-Match persistence. Recovery/export
  retains the interval-start boundary even after the player finishes before a
  rival worker. No-op multi-advance stops rather than counting free intervals.
- Imported active snapshots share the same namespace/runtime-policy gate as new
  activation. Structural mode suppresses legacy finance/event/board effects;
  supported default and dormant careers retain their previous behaviour.
- The existing season-hook registry receives one frozen completed-season context,
  then the prototype awaits setup. Promotion, next-season construction and normal
  paid entry remain dependent work.
- A competition that cannot finish (offered, qualified or entered) and a background
  result whose commit fails are closed with a logged fallback, and the interval
  still advances. Loading a save already in that state applies the same closure.
  A later round that is still pending stops with a message; drawing that round
  stays with #575.
- Typecheck/build and 191 focused integration checks passed. A separate same-tool
  pass ran 126 calendar/metadata/recovery tests and checked all five material
  review fixes. Full-suite/CI/browser evidence follows; this is not the required
  different-tool approval.
