- Authored AU and UK profiles retain existing club identities and construct one
  persistent 24-club/480-player world, with three disjoint eight-club divisions,
  168 regular fixtures and 24 preseason trial fixtures. Rating targets remain
  prototype inputs; no annual budgets or measured balance are claimed.
- Format v27 stores world membership and profile identity against canonical
  league instances. Supported dormant careers upgrade without constructing a
  world. World calendars, stage dates and active/awaiting-season references are

  finals only from stored sporting results. Division sizes, regular dates,

  ten-round top-two/top-four and eight-club top-eight profiles passed.

  auto-signing/market refresh is suppressed in this structural prototype. The
  normal entry UI, finance, promotion and next-season setup remain dependent work.
- Typecheck, 178 focused tests and 104 save-format tests passed. A separate
  same-tool review ran 159 tests and verified the future-finals calendar import
  fix; no material findings remain within that scope. Full suite/build and PR CI
  follow. This is not the required different-tool checker approval.

## #575 — Open Cup draw and sporting integration

- Fresh structural careers enter the player by default when their authored
  profile has enough free competition dates. Explicit decline leaves the other
  clubs in the event and the player's league unchanged; an explicit entry into
  a profile without a valid window is rejected before setup changes state.
- The default 24-club event saves its 32-slot seeded draw and independent finite
  tie order, with eight implicit byes, 23 actual matches and one champion. Reduced
  fields use the same topology and right-aligned saved dates. Byes never create
  played results, match stats, XP or monetary awards.
- The existing canonical finaliser remains the sole match-result owner. Existing
  pre-match/post-results hooks prepare known opponents, including reload catch-up.
  The saved bracket and completed fixtures expose stable round/title outcome IDs;
  separate idempotent payout receipts remain owned by #576, with no retroactive
  claim from these unpaid developer snapshots.
- Load/import policy rejects a changed draw, conflicting/future invalid dates,
  undrawn active cup or an already missed round before gameplay can fail later.
  Calendar-only cups work without optional regional-world metadata.
- The next round is drawn from the saved bracket once the previous round is
  complete, including on the week that round is due, and before
  `settleStructuralCalendar` runs. A cup that is progressing normally finishes
  on played results: recovery does not forfeit those ties or withdraw the cup.
  `SAVE_VERSION` stays 27.
- Typecheck and 50 dedicated tests passed; a 100-test combined calendar/division/
  cup run passed before the final worldless-calendar guard fix, which has its
  own regression. Full suite/build, bounded review, browser evidence and PR CI
  follow. Normal offers and financial rewards remain separate work.
