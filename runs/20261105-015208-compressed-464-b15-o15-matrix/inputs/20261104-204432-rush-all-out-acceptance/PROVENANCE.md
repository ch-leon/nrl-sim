# Pooling provenance

No matches were generated by the pooling script. Each source is a completed
live-sim harness run; its plan, original metadata, report and raw JSON are under
`inputs/`. Every seed is included once in each orientation, with no gaps or
overlaps. The plan can rerun the entire consecutive seed range directly.

For group i, recover M2_i = SE_i² × n_i × (n_i − 1). With pooled mean m,
M2 = Σ[M2_i + n_i × (mean_i − m)²], and SE = sqrt(M2 / [N × (N − 1)]).
Tracked points share and per-side counts use trackedSeeds; legacy counts use
seeds per written orientation. Rates and legs are weighted by their sample
counts; wrestle seconds are weighted by the number of wrestles.
