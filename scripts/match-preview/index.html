How to compare
  1. Turn your phone sideways, then press ▶. Keep speed at ×1.
  2. Watch a full match. The panel shows time watched for each half; pauses and half-time are excluded.
  3. Use Restart to repeat seed 1. The + panel lets you choose another seed; use the same seed in all three versions.

All versions use two 2:30 match-clock halves, equal 60-rated squads, a four-player bench and a 68m-wide field. The clock and viewing time differ because stoppages run faster and key moments slow down.

The retreat fix is included: non-markers must reach 10m back, or their goal line, before rushing in. Check both directions after a tackle and after a handover.

Experimental balance: ordinary kicking distances, fatigue and substitution rules are unchanged. The 60m version disables 40/20 because its two zones meet. These builds are for your review before any merge.
